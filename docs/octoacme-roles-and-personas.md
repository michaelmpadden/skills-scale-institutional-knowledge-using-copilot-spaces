# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## Technical Leads

### Role Summary
Technical Leads guide implementation decisions and help the development team deliver a coherent, maintainable solution.

### Responsibilities
- Translate product requirements into a technical approach and identify technical dependencies
- Guide design and code reviews and support developers with complex implementation work
- Surface technical risks, estimates, and trade-offs early
- Coordinate with QA/Testing to make solutions testable and address technical quality issues

### Decision-Making Scope
- Decide or recommend implementation details and technical standards within the team's agreed architecture
- Escalate changes affecting scope, commitments, or cross-team architecture to the Project Manager and relevant product or engineering leads

### Interactions
- Partner with Product Managers to clarify requirements and explain technical trade-offs
- Give Project Managers estimates, dependency updates, and early risk signals
- Coordinate implementation and reviews with Developers, and testability and defect resolution with QA/Testing
- Explain technical options and constraints to Stakeholders with the Project Manager or Product Manager

### Goals
- Keep implementation aligned with product needs and architectural standards
- Reduce technical risk and unblock the development team

### Typical Communication
- Technical designs and architecture discussions
- Estimates, dependency updates, and code review feedback

---

## QA / Testing Leads

### Role Summary
QA / Testing Leads coordinate quality practices so the team can verify that delivered work meets acceptance criteria and is ready for release.

### Responsibilities
- Define the test approach and identify coverage needs, environments, and quality risks
- Coordinate test execution, defect triage, and verification of fixes
- Report test results, unresolved defects, and release-quality concerns

### Decision-Making Scope
- Recommend whether quality criteria are met and whether risks require mitigation
- Escalate unmet acceptance criteria or release-blocking defects to the Project Manager and Product Manager; do not change product scope or accept business risk on their behalf

### Interactions
- Align acceptance criteria and quality expectations with Product Managers
- Coordinate test timing and release readiness with Project Managers
- Work with Developers and Technical Leads on testability, defect fixes, and prevention of regressions
- Communicate quality status and known limitations to Stakeholders through the Project Manager or Product Manager

### Goals
- Find important defects early and provide clear evidence of product quality
- Make release risks and remaining quality gaps visible

### Typical Communication
- Test plans, test results, and defect reports
- Quality and release-readiness updates

---

## Release Managers

### Role Summary
Release Managers coordinate release readiness and deployment handoffs so changes reach users safely and stakeholders know what to expect.

### Responsibilities
- Coordinate release scope, schedule, dependencies, and readiness checkpoints
- Verify that release requirements are tracked, including testing, release notes, and rollback plans
- Coordinate deployment communications and post-release handoffs

### Decision-Making Scope
- Coordinate the release schedule and recommend go / no-go based on readiness evidence
- Escalate unresolved quality, operational, or scope risks to the Project Manager and Product Manager; business owners make decisions to accept risk or change scope

### Interactions
- Align release scope and user-facing notes with Product Managers
- Coordinate milestones, dependencies, and stakeholder updates with Project Managers
- Confirm implementation and deployment readiness with Developers and Technical Leads, and test status with QA/Testing
- Share release timing, outcomes, and known issues with Stakeholders, coordinating messaging with the Project Manager and Product Manager

### Goals
- Make releases predictable and reduce deployment risk
- Ensure clear ownership of release readiness and post-release communication

### Typical Communication
- Release plans, readiness checklists, and deployment updates
- Release notes and post-release status
